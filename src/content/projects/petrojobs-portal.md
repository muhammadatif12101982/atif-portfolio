## Overview

An enterprise portal solution developed for the oil and gas sector, providing a centralized platform for organizational information, content, and user-facing services.

## Challenge

The organization required a centralized digital platform capable of organizing enterprise information and delivering a consistent experience for users across the business.

## Solution

The portal was implemented using Microsoft technologies with SharePoint providing content and collaboration capabilities, supported by custom .NET development where required.

The solution combined enterprise content management with custom application functionality to address business-specific requirements.

## Engineering Focus

- Enterprise portal architecture
- SharePoint development
- ASP.NET and C#
- SQL Server
- Custom web development
- JavaScript
- HTML and CSS
- Enterprise content management

## Outcome

The project delivered a centralized enterprise portal that brought business information and services together on a unified platform while providing a foundation for continued digital improvements.
