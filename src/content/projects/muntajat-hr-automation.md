## Overview

An HR automation initiative focused on streamlining employee-related business processes and reducing manual administrative activities.

## Challenge

HR processes involved repetitive manual activities and required a structured approach to managing information, approvals, and workflow-driven business operations.

## Solution

The solution used SharePoint and workflow automation capabilities to digitize and streamline HR processes.

Custom development and enterprise application components were used where required to support business-specific requirements.

## Engineering Focus

- HR process automation
- SharePoint development
- Workflow design
- Nintex workflow automation
- ASP.NET and C#
- SQL Server
- Business process digitization
- Enterprise application development

## Outcome

The solution helped streamline HR-related processes by replacing manual activities with structured, workflow-driven business processes and centralized information management.
