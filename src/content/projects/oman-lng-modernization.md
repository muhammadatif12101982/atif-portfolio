## Overview

A SharePoint migration and modernization initiative for Oman LNG, focused on improving the enterprise content platform and modernizing existing SharePoint capabilities.

## Challenge

The environment required modernization while preserving existing enterprise content, configurations, and business continuity.

## Solution

The initiative involved migration planning, SharePoint platform modernization, content migration, validation, and post-migration verification.

PowerShell and migration tooling were used to support repeatable administration and migration activities.

## Engineering Focus

- SharePoint platform modernization
- Enterprise content migration
- Migration planning and validation
- PowerShell automation
- ShareGate
- Microsoft 365
- Data integrity and operational continuity

## Outcome

The project modernized the SharePoint environment while maintaining the integrity and availability of enterprise content and providing a stronger foundation for future Microsoft 365 adoption.
