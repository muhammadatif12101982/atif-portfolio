## Overview

A Microsoft 365 modernization initiative focused on transforming existing SharePoint-based business solutions and moving enterprise workloads toward modern SharePoint Online and Power Platform capabilities.

## Challenge

The existing environment contained legacy SharePoint solutions and business processes that required modernization while maintaining business continuity and improving the user experience.

## Solution

The modernization involved migrating and redesigning enterprise solutions using SharePoint Online, SPFx, React, Power Apps, and Power Automate.

Custom functionality was redeveloped using modern client-side technologies while business processes were automated through Power Platform.

## Engineering Focus

- SharePoint Online modernization
- SPFx and React development
- TypeScript-based client-side solutions
- Power Apps development
- Power Automate workflow automation
- Microsoft 365 integration
- Enterprise application modernization
- User experience improvement

## Outcome

The initiative delivered more than 15 applications and workflows while improving adoption of modern Microsoft 365 solutions by approximately 30%.
