## Overview

An enterprise document management solution designed to centralize organizational documents and improve the management of business information.

## Challenge

The organization required a structured platform for managing enterprise documents and supporting controlled business processes around document creation, storage, and access.

## Solution

SharePoint was used as the foundation for enterprise document management, with custom .NET development and workflow capabilities used to support organization-specific requirements.

The solution provided centralized content management and a structured platform for managing business documents.

## Engineering Focus

- Enterprise document management
- SharePoint architecture and development
- Document libraries and information management
- Workflow-driven processes
- ASP.NET and C#
- SQL Server
- Custom application development
- Enterprise content management

## Outcome

The project provided a centralized document management platform that improved the organization of enterprise content and established a structured foundation for document-centric business processes.
