## Overview

An enterprise digital solution developed for the Abu Dhabi Department of Transport to support organizational information management and business processes.

## Challenge

The organization required an enterprise platform capable of supporting structured information management and business-specific digital requirements.

## Solution

The solution was developed using Microsoft enterprise technologies, combining SharePoint capabilities with custom .NET development to address the organization's requirements.

Custom application components were developed where standard platform functionality required extension.

## Engineering Focus

- Enterprise solution development
- SharePoint
- ASP.NET and C#
- SQL Server
- Custom application development
- Information management
- Business process support
- Microsoft technology stack

## Outcome

The project delivered an enterprise digital solution aligned with organizational information-management and business-process requirements while providing a maintainable Microsoft-based platform.
