## Overview

An enterprise SharePoint intranet initiative focused on providing a centralized platform for organizational information, collaboration, and employee-facing services.

## Challenge

The organization required an effective enterprise intranet experience that could provide structured access to information while supporting modern SharePoint capabilities and business requirements.

## Solution

The solution used SharePoint as the foundation for the enterprise intranet, with custom functionality and user experiences implemented using modern client-side technologies.

The platform was designed to improve information accessibility, support collaboration, and provide a maintainable foundation for future enhancements.

## Engineering Focus

- Enterprise SharePoint architecture
- Intranet development
- SharePoint customization
- SPFx development
- React-based components
- TypeScript and JavaScript
- Microsoft 365 integration
- User experience modernization

## Outcome

The project delivered an enterprise SharePoint intranet platform designed to centralize organizational information and provide a modern foundation for internal collaboration and employee services.
